Before controls, a map. This module explains what makes AI systems a distinct
security problem, gives you a repeatable way to threat-model one, and introduces
the public frameworks your work will be measured against.

By the end of it you should be able to point at any AI feature and answer two
questions: **what can influence the model**, and **what can the model influence**.
